The Four Hogwarts Houses

Which House Will Choose You?

At Hogwarts, students are sorted into one of four houses, each with its own rich history, values, traditions, and legendary members.

🏰 Explore the Four Houses

🦁 Gryffindor

"Where dwell the brave at heart."

About Gryffindor

Founded by Godric Gryffindor, this house is home to witches and wizards who value bravery, courage, determination, and chivalry. Gryffindors are known for standing up for what is right, protecting others, and facing even the greatest dangers with confidence and honor.


🛡 Founder: Godric Gryffindor

🦁 Mascot: Lion

❤️ Colors: Scarlet & Gold

⭐ Traits: Courage, Bravery, Nerve, Chivalry

🪄 Head of House: Professor Minerva McGonagall

Famous Gryffindors

Harry Potter

The Boy Who Lived and one of the bravest Gryffindors in Hogwarts history.

Hermione Granger

Brilliant, courageous, and one of the greatest witches of her generation.

Ron Weasley

Loyal friend whose courage and heart helped save the wizarding world.

🦡 Hufflepuff

"Those patient Hufflepuffs are true and unafraid of toil."

About Hufflepuff

Founded by Helga Hufflepuff, this house is known for welcoming students who are kind, hardworking, patient, and loyal. Hufflepuffs believe that every witch and wizard deserves a fair chance to succeed, making the house one of the friendliest and most supportive at Hogwarts.


🛡 Founder: Helga Hufflepuff

🦡 Mascot: Badger

💛 Colors: Yellow & Black

⭐ Traits: Loyalty, Patience, Hard Work, Fairness

🌿 Head of House: Professor Pomona Sprout

Famous Hufflepuffs

Cedric Diggory

Triwizard Champion known for his courage, humility, and fairness.

Nymphadora Tonks

A brave Auror and member of the Order of the Phoenix.

Pomona Sprout

Head of Hufflepuff House and beloved Herbology professor.

🦅 Ravenclaw

"Wit beyond measure is man's greatest treasure."

About Ravenclaw

Founded by Rowena Ravenclaw, this house is home to students who possess intelligence, wisdom, creativity, and an endless curiosity for learning. Ravenclaws are independent thinkers who enjoy solving problems, discovering new ideas, and expanding their knowledge through study and imagination.


🛡 Founder: Rowena Ravenclaw

🦅 Mascot: Eagle

💙 Colors: Blue & Bronze

⭐ Traits: Intelligence, Wisdom, Creativity, Learning

🪄 Head of House: Professor Filius Flitwick

Famous Ravenclaws

Luna Lovegood

Known for her creativity, kindness, and unique perspective, Luna proved that wisdom often comes from seeing the world differently.

Cho Chang

A skilled Quidditch Seeker whose determination and talent made her one of Ravenclaw's most respected students.

Garrick Ollivander

The legendary wandmaker whose unmatched knowledge of wandlore has helped countless witches and wizards find their perfect wand.

🐍 Slytherin

"Those cunning folk use any means to achieve their ends."

About Slytherin

Founded by Salazar Slytherin, this house is known for producing ambitious, resourceful, determined, and confident witches and wizards. Slytherins are natural leaders who possess great strategic thinking and are driven to achieve their goals. Throughout Hogwarts' history, many influential figures have proudly belonged to Slytherin House.


🛡 Founder: Salazar Slytherin

🐍 Mascot: Serpent

💚 Colors: Green & Silver

⭐ Traits: Ambition, Leadership, Resourcefulness, Determination

🧪 Head of House: Professor Severus Snape

Famous Slytherins

Draco Malfoy

A proud Slytherin student recognized for his ambition, confidence, and exceptional magical ability.

Severus Snape

Hogwarts' legendary Potions Master whose bravery and sacrifices played a vital role in defeating Voldemort.

Merlin

One of the greatest wizards in history, remembered for his extraordinary wisdom, magical achievements, and lasting legacy.
