Core Magical Subjects

Every Hogwarts student studies a variety of magical disciplines taught by some of the greatest witches and wizards in history.

🧪 Potions

Professor: Severus Snape

Difficulty: ★★★★★

Classroom: Dungeon 5

Signature Spell: Felix Felicis Brewing

House Points: +15 per assignment

Learn to brew magical potions, antidotes, elixirs, and powerful magical mixtures using rare wizarding ingredients.

✨ Charms

Professor: Filius Flitwick

Difficulty: ★★★★☆

Classroom: Third Floor

Signature Spell: Wingardium Leviosa

House Points: +10 per spell

Master spells that enchant objects, create magical effects, and improve everyday wizarding life.

🌿 Herbology

Professor: Pomona Sprout

Difficulty: ★★★☆☆

Classroom: Greenhouse Three

Signature Spell: Herb Care Magic

House Points: +10 per project

Discover magical plants, healing herbs, venomous tentacula, and the fascinating flora of the wizarding world.

🛡️ Defense Against the Dark Arts

Professor: Remus Lupin

Difficulty: ★★★★★

Classroom: Third Floor

Signature Spell: Expecto Patronum

House Points: +20 per duel

Learn magical defense, dueling techniques, and protection against dark creatures and curses.

🔄 Transfiguration

Professor: Minerva McGonagall

Difficulty: ★★★★★

Classroom: First Floor

Signature Spell: Human Transfiguration

House Points: +20 for excellence

Transform animals, objects, and even yourself using one of the most advanced branches of magic.

🌌 Astronomy

Professor: Aurora Sinistra

Difficulty: ★★★☆☆

Classroom: Astronomy Tower

Signature Topic: Celestial Magic

House Points: +8 per observation

Observe stars, planets, constellations, and magical celestial events.

🐉 Care of Magical Creatures

Professor: Rubeus Hagrid

Difficulty: ★★★★☆

Classroom: Forbidden Forest Grounds

Signature Creature: Hippogriff

House Points: +15 for bravery

Meet dragons, unicorns, hippogriffs, nifflers, and many extraordinary magical creatures.

🔮 Divination

Professor: Sybill Trelawney

Difficulty: ★★★☆☆

Classroom: North Tower

Signature Method: Crystal Ball Reading

House Points: +10 per prediction

Explore crystal balls, tea leaves, dreams, and prophecies to uncover glimpses of the future.

Graduation Requirements

🎓 Become a Qualified Witch or Wizard


✅ Pass all O.W.L. Examinations

✅ Complete every Core Subject

✅ Earn at least 500 House Points

✅ Participate in Magical Activities

✅ Demonstrate courage, wisdom, loyalty, or ambition

✅ Follow the Hogwarts Code of Conduct
