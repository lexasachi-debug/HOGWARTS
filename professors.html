Albus Percival Wulfric Brian Dumbledore

Headmaster of Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry

Widely regarded as the greatest wizard of the modern age, Albus Dumbledore dedicated his life to protecting the wizarding world from darkness. His remarkable wisdom, kindness, humility, and unmatched magical ability inspired countless generations of witches and wizards.

🪄 Master of the Elder Wand
